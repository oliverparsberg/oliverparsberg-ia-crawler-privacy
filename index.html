Privacy Policy — IA Screenshot Crawler for Figma

Last updated: November 26, 2026

IA Screenshot Crawler for Figma ("the Extension") does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, browsing history, analytics, or user credentials with external servers or third parties.

Local Processing and Storage

  • All webpage navigation, scrolling, screenshot capturing, and information architecture grouping occur locally in the user's browser.
  • Captured screenshots and metadata files (ia-data.json) are saved directly to the user's local device via the browser's downloads folder.
  • Extension preferences (such as the last export folder path) are stored locally using chrome.storage.local.

Permissions

  • debugger: Used exclusively to capture full-page screenshots (Page.captureBeyondViewport) of the active tab during a crawl.
  • scripting & tabs: Used to read page navigation links, scroll pages to load lazy content, and navigate internal URLs during a crawl.
  • downloads & storage: Used to save image/JSON files locally and remember export paths.
  • <all_urls>: Required because users audit arbitrary websites chosen at runtime.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact the developer listed on the Chrome Web Store listing.
